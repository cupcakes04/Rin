### One real data bug left in `9c6c` (worth a 1-minute fix before M5)
- **Mislabelled `crowd` box on `2018-03-12 10:10 G330` (`m562f09e2fe07883d`), `frame_idx = 7455`:**
  - On every other frame of that clip (`2295`, `3240`, `3300`, `6225`, `7830`, `7995`, `8760`), the far bleacher box (`~[1001..1914, 219..483]`) is labelled `crowd`.
  - On frame `7455`, `[1001, 255, 1869, 458]` (`868 × 203 px`) was accidentally left as class **`person`** instead of `crowd`.
  - Because its class is `person`, `human.annotate` did **not** strip the **31** individual `person` boxes inside it — so M5 will score both that `868 × 203` bleacher box *and* all 31 noisy crowd people inside it as real `person` targets. (Checked all other `person` boxes containing ≥ 3 boxes; `7455` is the only mislabelled `crowd` box in `9c6c`.)

### Small bugs & nits introduced in `8d77d5f`
1. **backup_human_runs.sh (L15-26) breaks on relative destination paths:** line 17 runs `mkdir -p "$dest"` in the caller's cwd, then line 18 runs `cd "$store"`, then line 26 writes `tar czf "$dest/..."` — so `bash dev/scripts/backup_human_runs.sh backups` fails unless `dest` is resolved to an absolute path before `cd "$store"`.
2. **viewer/__init__.py (L70-71) forgot to guard `draw="marks_..."`:** line 70 rejects `draw.startswith(("run_", "hints_"))`, not `"marks_"`. Passing `draw="marks_suspects"` alongside `marks={"suspects": ...}` silently overwrites the read-only `marks_suspects` field in viewer/job.py (L42).
3. **Minor quirks in human/suspects.py:**
   - `_split_masks` (L130-135) decodes **all** masks in `masks` (including 0.1–0.5 detections) without filtering by `above`, making `suspects(..., masks=sam)` noticeably slower than needed on full SAM3 runs.
   - `same_spot` and `duplicate` (L81-106) ignore `class`, so running `suspects` on `9c6c` mixes `crowd` and `person` boxes together.
   - Adding `pycocotools` to core pyproject.toml (L14) under NumPy 2 introduces 2 `DeprecationWarning`s in pytest (`pycocotools/mask.py:91`). Harmless, just worth knowing.
4. **Two tiny doc slips:**
   - dev/notes/state.md (L23) says *"the store holds 10 runs"*; `workspace/store/` actually holds **11** runs.
   - dev/research/m3_pilot/README.md (L42-46) updated the table to 11 missed / 29 wrong (for detector boxes), while lines 42 and 46 still read *"The 12 people SAM3 missed"* and *"The 30 SAM3 boxes"* (matching the older mask-box contact sheets, just slightly inconsistent with the table above).
