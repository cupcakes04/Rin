## 1. Critical M5 experiment confounds & measurement bugs

### 1.1 Fixed `epochs=50` with Ultralytics' `nbs=64` gradient accumulation severely undertrains small budgets — confounding D-17's "half the labels" test (Critical)
- **Where:** `src/rin/servants/yolo/job.py:59-65`, `dev/notes/decisions.md:98` (D-40), `dev/research/m4_first_curve/README.md:27-30,37-38`, `dev/notes/milestones/README.md:51-52`, `workspace/store/runs/20261127-15*-yolo.train-*/files/{args.yaml,results.csv}`.
- **What actually happens in Ultralytics 8.4.220** (checked via `inspect.getsource` on `BaseTrainer._build_train_pipeline`, `BaseTrainer._do_train`, and `BaseTrainer.build_optimizer` in `src/rin/servants/yolo/.venv`):
  1. `yolo/job.py:59-65` fixes `epochs=50`, `batch=8`, leaving Ultralytics' defaults `nbs=64` (nominal batch size) and `optimizer="auto"`.
  2. Ultralytics computes `self.accumulate = max(round(nbs / batch), 1) = 8` (`_build_train_pipeline:22`), stepping the optimizer only once every 8 micro-batches (`ni - last_opt_step >= self.accumulate`, `_do_train:73`). Because `iterations <= 10,000`, `optimizer="auto"` silently overrides `lr0=0.01` with **AdamW at `lr0 = 0.002`** (`build_optimizer:29-31`).
  3. Because COCO has `nc=80` and `export.yolo` has `nc=1`, the 1-class classification head is re-initialized from scratch and needs enough gradient steps to converge.
  4. At fixed `epochs=50`, total optimizer steps shrink linearly with budget `N` — **10× fewer updates at `N=100` than `N=1,000`, and 30× fewer at M5's planned `N=25`**:

     | Budget `N` | Batches / epoch (`batch=8`) | Batches in 50 epochs | Approx. AdamW steps (`accumulate=8`) |
     |---|---|---|---|
     | 25 *(M5 planned)* | 4 | 200 | **~25** (~1 step every 2 epochs) |
     | 50 *(M5 planned)* | 7 | 350 | **~44** |
     | 100 | 13 | 650 | **~81** |
     | 250 | 32 | 1,600 | **~200** |
     | 500 | 63 | 3,150 | **~394** |
     | 1,000 | 125 | 6,250 | **~781** |

- **Direct proof in M4's own `results.csv`:**
  - At epoch 50, `N=100` (`4177`) ends at **`train/box_loss = 0.909`, `train/cls_loss = 0.489`**, whereas `N=1,000` (`1dac`) ends at **`train/box_loss = 0.715`, `train/cls_loss = 0.351`**. A 10× *smaller* training set normally has *lower* training loss (easier to fit), not 27–39% higher. `N=100` simply ran out of optimizer steps.
  - On `val`, `N=1,000` already hits `0.623` after **epoch 1** (~16 AdamW steps) and `0.673` at epoch 10 (~156 steps), while `N=100` is still crawling at `0.353` (ep 1), `0.489` (ep 10), `0.620` (ep 25), `0.658` (ep 41).
- **This also misdiagnoses M4's empty-frame false-positive drop (`m4_first_curve/README.md:38`):**
  - Querying `workspace/store` for `random` seed 0 (`20261127-132314-curate.select-5da9`) against SAM3 (`5477`, `score >= 0.5`): **all four budgets (`N=100, 250, 500, 1,000`) contain ZERO empty (0-box) frames** (`<=5` people: 1, 3, 5, 10 frames respectively).
  - Yet `fp_per_empty_frame` dropped **11×** from `3.22` (`N=100`) to `0.30` (`N=1,000`) — reaching `0.00` on G299 03-13, G299 03-15, and G330 03-15. That drop did not come from `N=1,000` seeing empty gym frames; it came mostly from `N=100` stopping after ~81 optimizer steps with an unconverged classification head (354 empty-frame FPs at median score `0.597`, mean `25×70 px` across the middle bleacher strip).
- **Why this breaks M5:** D-17 compares curated `N` frames against random `2N` frames. Fixing `epochs=50` gives `N=25` or `N=50` half as many gradient updates as `2N`, handicapping small budgets by optimization steps rather than data quality — right in the `N <= 250` region where M5 intends to decide D-17!
- **Fix before M5:** Match **optimizer steps** (or train small budgets to convergence by scaling `epochs` inversely with `N` up to a cap, or lowering `nbs` to `batch` so `accumulate=1` with a tuned `lr0` and checking convergence at `N=25` and `N=50`). Note that `N=25` at 200 epochs is still faster than `N=1,000` at 50 epochs.

### 1.2 `yolo.predict` caps detections at `max_det=100` across all 80 COCO classes *before* filtering `classes`, silently depressing the `N=0` stock baseline (High)
- **Where:** `src/rin/servants/yolo/job.py:39-45`, `src/rin/servants/yolo/__init__.py:182`, `dev/research/m4_first_curve/README.md:25-26`, `workspace/store/runs/20261127-152517-yolo.predict-aef6`.
- **What happens:**
  - `yolo.predict` writes `classes` into `spec.json` (`yolo/__init__.py:176`), **never passes `classes` to `model.predict()`** (`job.py:39-42`), and filters `classes` in Python *after* Ultralytics already truncated to `max_det=100` (`yolo/__init__.py:182`).
  - Ultralytics' `nms.non_max_suppression` (`end2end=True` for YOLO26, verified in `src/rin/servants/yolo/.venv`) explicitly filters `classes` *before* slicing `[:max_det]` when `classes` is passed.
- **Measured in `workspace/store` (`20261127-152517-yolo.predict-aef6`, stock 1280):**
  - Stock 1280 emitted only **5,507** `person` detections across all 300 test frames (vs **12,271–23,752** for the 1-class fine-tuned students), never once reaching 100 (`max = 65`).
  - On **178 of 187** non-empty frames, stock 1280's lowest `person` confidence is `> 0.01` (not `0.001`), and on **32 frames** it cuts off above `0.05`.
  - On the 60-person frame, stock 1280 emitted only **44** `person` detections (cutoff score **`0.0622`**) because **56 of its 100 `max_det` slots were taken by non-person COCO classes** (`chair`, `backpack`, `handbag`, `bench`, etc.). On 12 non-empty frames, stock 1280 output fewer `person` boxes than there are ground-truth people.
- **Impact:** M4's stock baseline (`mAP50-95 = 0.599`, `mAP_small = 0.090`, `AR100 = 0.658`) is artificially depressed, overstating the gain from fine-tuning (`+0.020` at `N=100`).
- **Fix:** In `yolo/job.py:predict`, resolve `spec["classes"]` against `model.names` into integer class IDs (raising loudly if a class name doesn't exist in `model.names`) and pass `classes=class_ids` to `model.predict(...)`. Re-run the stock baseline for M5.
- *(Related note on `max_det=100` for fine-tuned students:* on crowded frames with `crowd` ignore boxes, up to 69 SAM3 people sit on one frame; `n=1000` (`bc99`) hits `max_det=100` on 51 frames with cutoff score up to `0.020` because ~40–60 of its 100 slots go to people inside ignored `crowd` boxes. Much smaller than the stock bug, but worth noting if `max_det` is ever tightened.)*

### 1.4 `kmeans_stratified` with `clusters=50` per camera breaks D-28 when M5 lowers budgets to `N = 25 / 50` (Medium–High)
- **Where:** `src/rin/curate/select.py:99-101,149-153`, `dev/notes/usage.md:226,231`, `dev/notes/milestones/README.md:51`, `dev/research/m4_selection/README.md:13,40`.
- **What happens:** M4 chose `clusters=50` per camera (`100` clusters total) to match `min(budgets) = 100`. M5 plans to lower budgets to `25 / 50 / 100 / 250 / 500` (`milestones/README.md:51`). Reusing M4's `clusters=50` rankings (`2d06`, `d369`, `c75a`) means:

## 2. Bugs and safety gaps in current code

### 2.1 `yolo.predict` ignores `classes` inside Ultralytics and silently accepts unknown class names (High)
- **Where:** `src/rin/servants/yolo/job.py:39-45`, `src/rin/servants/yolo/__init__.py:182`.
- Besides the `max_det=100` truncation on multi-class models (§1.2), filtering `d["class"] in classes` only in Python means a typo like `classes=["Person"]` silently produces **0 detections** instead of failing loudly against `model.names` (violating `CLAUDE.md:102`).

### 2.2 `Dataset._without_duplicates` suppresses overlapping boxes across *different* classes (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/dataset.py:119-138` (line `125`: `boxes.groupby(["media_id", "frame_idx"])`).
- **Reproduced:** Passing two identical boxes with `class="person"` (score `0.9`) and `class="backpack"` (score `0.8`) into `_without_duplicates(df, 0.7)` returned `[True, False]`, silently deleting `backpack`.
- **Fix:** Group by `["media_id", "frame_idx", "class"]` so NMS-like duplicate removal only compares boxes of the same class.

### 2.3 Missing `split` guards in `curate.select` and `export.yolo` (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/curate/select.py:68-77`, `src/rin/export/yolo.py:44-46`, vs `dev/notes/data_model.md:74` (*"Selection only reads `pool` media"*) and `dev/notes/decisions.md:54` (D-13).
- **What happens:**
  1. `curate.select` never checks `d.split == 'pool'`, despite `data_model.md:74` stating it as a rule. Passing `val` or `test` frames succeeds silently.
  2. `export.yolo` checks only that train and val share no `media_id` (`export/yolo.py:44-46`), never refusing `split == 'test'` in train or val. One swapped variable in an M5 script can silently train or validate on the test set.
  3. Also, `curate.select(..., method="random")` requires an `emb` run and fails if any frame lacks an embedding (`select.py:59,78-80`), even though `random` never uses `emb`. M5's planned *"random over raw sampled frames"* baseline (`milestones/README.md:46`) has 226 motion-dropped frames that were never embedded, so `curate.select` can't run it unless `emb` is optional for `method="random"`.

### 2.4 `yolo.train`'s `os.chdir(Path(spec["model"]).parent)` will write `yolo26n.pt` into an immutable run folder if fine-tuning from a student (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/servants/yolo/job.py:53-55`, `dev/notes/models/README.md:66`.
- **What happens:** `job.py:55` changes `cwd` to the checkpoint's parent folder so Ultralytics' `check_amp()` finds `yolo26n.pt` there instead of downloading it into the repo. That works only while `model` is in `workspace/checkpoints/yolo/`. If M6 (or any round-2 run) fine-tunes from a previous `yolo.train` run's `files/best.pt`, `check_amp()` will download `yolo26n.pt` directly into that **immutable finished run folder** `runs/<prev_id>/files/` (violating D-02/D-23).
- **Fix:** Run in `out_dir` (which is temporary and cleaned up) with a symlink/hard link to `yolo26n.pt` if present, or pass `amp=True` / isolate Ultralytics' working dir in `out_dir`.

### 2.5 `evaluate.coco` crashes on 0 predictions instead of scoring `mAP = 0.0` (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/evaluate/coco.py:65-66`.
- **What happens:** `if not dets: raise ValueError(...)`. A poorly converged tiny-budget student (`N=25`) or early checkpoint that emits 0 boxes crashes an unattended M5 sweep instead of recording `mAP = 0.0` and `fp_per_empty_frame = 0.0`.

### 2.6 `export.yolo` mixes `train` and `val` into one `frame_sets` and one `instances` table (Low–Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/export/yolo.py:74-97`.
- **What happens:** `train` frames get `rank = 0..N-1` and `val` frames get `rank = None` inside the same `frame_sets` table, and both splits' boxes go into one `instances` table. As a result, `len(exp)` is `len(train) + len(val)` (1,308 for a 1,000-frame export), `m.dataset(frames=exp, top=N)` fails (`"has frames without a rank"`), and `exp.instances()` gives no split column.

### 2.7 Minor validation and provenance gaps in `yolo` and `export.coco` (Low)
- **`yolo.predict` omits the `imgsz % 32` check** (`src/rin/servants/yolo/__init__.py:163-165` vs `yolo.train` at lines `66-67`).
- **Inconsistent `model` / `checkpoint` provenance in `yolo`:** `yolo.train` stores bare filename `checkpoint="yolo26s.pt"` (`yolo/__init__.py:72`) instead of a workspace-relative path (unlike `sam3.concept`, `sam3/__init__.py:79`) and accepts checkpoints outside `workspace/`. `yolo.predict` stores the `yolo.train` `run_id` in the `model` column and `"best.pt"` in `checkpoint` (`yolo/__init__.py:173`), contradicting `data_model.md:53-54` (`model` = model name, `checkpoint` = path).
- **Unrecorded runtime optimizer/LR in `yolo.train`:** `files/args.yaml` records `optimizer: auto, lr0: 0.01`, whereas Ultralytics actually ran `AdamW, lr0=0.002` (§1.1). If a future run crosses 10,000 iterations, Ultralytics silently switches to `MuSGD, lr0=0.01` without `config` or `args.yaml` showing the change.
- **`export.coco` omits duplicate class check:** `export.yolo:34` rejects duplicate entries in `classes`; `export.coco:26-30` does not (passing `["person", "person"]` silently collapses to `{ "person": 2 }`).
- **Raw `r.boxes.xyxyn` in `yolo/job.py:44`:** Ultralytics computes `xyxyn` in float32; any `1.0000001` rounding overshoot or degenerate zero-width box will fail `schema.py:_check_boxes` (`schema.py:143`) *after* GPU inference completes.

## 3. Lessons from `dev/rin_v1/` that v2 lacks or should revisit

1. **`1e-6` geometry jitter clamp (`dev/rin_v1/src/rin/data/core/recorder.py`, noted in `dev/notes/lessons/rin_v1.md:56`):** `lessons/rin_v1.md:56` explicitly flagged clamping float rounding jitter within `1e-6` while raising beyond it, and v1's `dev/rin_v1/src/rin/services/ground/yolo/helpers.py:49-52` clamped YOLO box outputs to `[0, 1]`. v2's `schema.py:_check_boxes` has zero tolerance while `yolo/job.py:44` passes raw float32 `xyxyn` (§2.7).
2. **Explicit optimizer & LR config vs `optimizer="auto"` (`dev/rin_v1/src/rin/services/ground/yolo/engine.py:324-326`):** v1 passed `lr0`, `lrf`, `optimizer`, `patience` explicitly instead of letting Ultralytics' `auto` heuristic and `nbs=64` silently alter the optimizer and step count across dataset sizes (§1.1).
3. **Foreground-aware RoI pooling (`dev/rin_v1/src/rin/data/transform/pooler.py:70-182`):** M4 already showed whole-frame DINOv3 `kmeans_stratified` cannot be told from random (`m4_selection/README.md:40`). Dropping *all* empty frames with a hard motion cutoff removes every negative background example from the training pool (§4.2).

## 4. Doubts about the experiment, data, and direction

### 4.1 Statistical power: 98.6% of test GT people sit in 3 crowded clips, and overall `mAP50-95` headroom may be smaller than the noise floor
- **Where:** `workspace/store/runs/20261127-094340-human.annotate-64f9`, `dev/research/m4_first_curve/README.md:23-30`, `dev/notes/milestones/README.md:40`.
- **Measured in `workspace/store`:**
  - Across the 300 test frames (`3,432` checked people):
    - `03-09`: `1,261` people (**G330 03-09 alone holds 1,052 people = 30.7% of all test GT boxes** in 34 frames from one 15-minute window).
    - `03-11`: `906` people (26.4%); `03-12`: `1,217` people (35.5%).
    - `03-13` + `03-15` (132 of 300 frames = 44% of the test set): only **48 people total (1.4% of GT boxes)** and 106 empty frames.
  - Overall `mAP50-95` moved only `+0.062` from M4's truncated stock baseline (`0.599`) to `N=1,000` (`0.661`) — and once §1.2's stock truncation is fixed, the true total headroom from `N=0` to `N=1,000` will likely be **~0.04–0.05 mAP** (and only **~0.03** between `N=0` and `N=250`).
  - Meanwhile, parked debt (`milestones/README.md:40`) already notes that temporal clustering in the test set makes 1–2 point differences unreadable.
- **Implication for M5:** Overall `mAP50-95` may simply lack the dynamic range on this gym to separate curation methods at 2× label savings, even if curation works. The metrics with real headroom are **`mAP_small`** (`0.090 → 0.277`, +0.19) and **`fp_per_empty_frame`** (`3.22 → 0.30`). Plan to report block-bootstrap confidence intervals (resampling by 5-minute clip, not individual frames) alongside seed spread.

### 4.2 Hard motion filtering (`motion >= 0.002`) hurts detector training by purging negative (empty-room) frames
- **Where:** `dev/research/m4_selection/pool_chain.py:24`, `dev/research/m4_first_curve/README.md:38`, `dev/notes/milestones/README.md:52`.
- Dropping every low-motion frame before embedding/dedupe guarantees the training pool has zero empty-room states — and on real 24/7 CCTV (which is mostly empty, across changing light), that will teach students to hallucinate whenever a room empties.
- **Alternative:** Instead of hard-dropping empty frames with `motion >= 0.002`, let `dedupe` collapse long empty stretches into a handful of distinct empty-background frames (one per lighting shift). That preserves empty negatives in the candidate pool at negligible cost.

### 4.3 Watch for a misleading `kcenter` win at low budgets (confounded by sparse frames & cleaner SAM3 boxes)
- **Where:** `workspace/store/runs/20261127-132314-curate.select-{5da9,8f97}`, `20261127-111139-sam3.concept-5477`.
- **Measured in `workspace/store` (seed 0, `N=25` / `50` / `100`):**
  - `kcenter` picks `45.1–47.0` people/frame vs `random`'s `50.0–51.1` (about 10% fewer boxes at `N=25–50`), **higher** mean SAM3 confidence (`0.773–0.777` vs `0.759–0.763`), **fewer** borderline `0.5–0.6` boxes (`12.2–13.6%` vs `15.0–16.2%`), and **more** sparse frames (`<=5` people: 3–4 frames at `N=50–100` vs `random`'s 0–1).
  - If `kcenter` beats `random` in M5 mainly on `fp_per_empty_frame`, check whether it won because it recovered the few sparse frames that `motion >= 0.002` nearly erased, rather than because whole-frame DINOv3 represents person diversity. Including **raw-frame random** (without `motion >= 0.002`) and **uniform-in-time** baselines (`milestones/README.md:46-47`) will separate the two effects cleanly.

### 4.4 Measure the SAM3 teacher ceiling on `export.coco-8bc8` before interpreting M5's saturation
- **Where:** `dev/research/m4_first_curve/README.md`, `workspace/store/runs/20261126-205940-sam3.concept-11ee`, `20261127-152506-export.coco-8bc8`.
- Both SAM3's test predictions (`11ee`) and the frozen COCO truth (`8bc8`) are already in the store. Running `evaluate.coco` on SAM3 (`11ee`, both raw and gated at `>= 0.5`) takes seconds and tells you the **teacher ceiling** (`mAP50-95`, `mAP_small`, `fp_per_empty_frame`) on the exact same scoring protocol. Without it, you cannot tell whether `N=1,000`'s `0.661` (`mAP_small = 0.277`) is still far below SAM3 or has already hit the ceiling of SAM3's pseudo-labels.
