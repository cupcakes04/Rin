## 1. Critical findings in the M3 test set and research verdicts

### 1.1 Automation bias left ~160 stationary bag/coat false positives (and 56 falsely non-empty frames) in the "checked" test set (Critical)
- **Where:** `workspace/store/runs/20261126-164727-human.annotate-71ed`, `dev/research/m3_checked_test_set/README.md:11-20,28-29`, `dev/research/m3_pilot/README.md:36-40,46`, `dev/notes/ideas/review_by_exception.md:23,32`.
- **What actually happened:** In `71ed`, **4,854 of 4,884 boxes (99.39%) are bit-for-bit identical to SAM3 > 0.5** (`20261126-130121-sam3.concept-7efc`). Across 300 frames reviewed in 14 minutes (2.8 s/frame on pass 1), only 43 SAM3 boxes were deleted (0.88%) and 30 added/moved (0.61%) — an order of magnitude fewer edits than the blind pilot's measured error rates (~7% misses, ~5–10% wrong/duplicate boxes, `m3_pilot/README.md:19,46`).
- **Concrete proof in the data and cached frames:**
  1. **2018-03-13 G330 (35 test frames):** In `workspace/store/frames/me855168a46d3cbfb/1680.jpg` — literally the empty-gym pilot example cited in `review_by_exception.md:23` (*"frame 1680 has nobody on it, yet SAM3 put 4 boxes above 0.5 there"*) — the gym is 100% empty, and SAM3 boxed 4 backpacks/coats on the left red bleachers (`[194,637,234,702]`, `[56,470,84,508]`, `[318,744,371,847]`, `[0,426,23,463]`). In `71ed`, the user deleted them on only **2** of the 35 frames (`ma35e9ba1c5128da5` frame `1680` and `me855168a46d3cbfb` frame `4485`), leaving **122 of 130 boxes on 03-13 G330** sitting on those same left-bleacher bags across the other 33 frames — including `me855168a46d3cbfb` frame `1680` itself! **31 frames** of 03-13 G330 have *only* those bag boxes in `71ed` (truly empty gym frames labelled as 3–4 people).
  2. **2018-03-15 G330 (41 test frames):** In `workspace/store/frames/m340508d3dc83b042/495.jpg` (and 37 other frames), SAM3 boxed a black jacket/bag at the bottom edge of the red bleachers (`[643, 1007, 745, 1080]`, also visible in `dev/research/m3_pilot/false_at_0.5.jpg` row 2 col 7, `72px s0.55`). Because **0 of 65 frames on 03-15 were edited** (`m3_checked_test_set/README.md:20`), all **38 of 60 boxes on 03-15 G330** are that single jacket/bag, turning **25 truly empty frames** into "1 person" frames.
  3. **On the 18 pilot frames themselves:** Only **2 of 18 frames** were edited at all in `71ed` (3 boxes deleted, 3 added). Every single sparse-frame false positive found in the pilot — `03-13 G330 1680` (4 wrong), `03-13 G330 5715` (3 wrong), `03-15 G330 6690` (1 wrong), `03-12 G299 4635` (2 missed, 4 wrong) — survived into `71ed` completely untouched.

  1. **Test set fails D-13:** There are ~111 truly empty frames, not 55, and at least **160 stationary non-person boxes** sit in `71ed` as ground-truth `person`. In M5, a good YOLO student that correctly predicts *nothing* on empty gyms gets penalized with 160 false negatives, while a student that imitates SAM3's bag hallucinations gets rewarded.
  2. **`review_by_exception.md:32` is off by >10×:** The headline *"trusting frames with 20 boxes or fewer would have spared 215 frames, 6 of them (1 in 36) with an error"* counted only frames the user *noticed*. ### 1.2 The "two careful passes disagree on a fifth of crowd people" finding (basis for D-34) is misdiagnosed (High)
- **Where:** `dev/research/m3_checked_test_set/README.md:24,30`, `dev/notes/decisions.md:86` (D-34), `dev/notes/state.md:26`.

  > *"On the 18 pilot frames the same person labelled twice: blind (pilot) and checked from SAM3. Matched at IoU 0.3, 169 people are in both, 11 only in the blind pass, 29 only in the checked one; nearly all differences are in crowds. Two careful passes disagree on about a fifth of the people in a dense crowd; in sparse frames they agree."*
- **What the store actually shows (running IoU ≥ 0.3 matching between `3bd2` and `71ed` on the 18 pilot frames):**
  - **195 of 198 boxes (98.5%)** in `71ed` on those 18 frames are untouched SAM3 > 0.5 outputs (vs `m3_pilot/README.md:19`: `168 matched, 12 only blind, 30 only SAM3` → literally shifted by 1 box to `169 / 11 / 29`). ### 1.3 Root cause of SAM3's "group / stretched boxes" is `mask_box_xyxy_px` spanning disconnected mask fragments — 102 remain in `71ed` and will corrupt M4 training (Critical)
- **Where:** `src/rin/servants/sam3/job.py:53`, `vendors/sam3_engine/src/sam3_engine/types.py:45-52`, `vendors/sam3_engine/src/sam3_engine/_coords.py:66-71`, `dev/research/m3_checked_test_set/README.md:22`, `dev/notes/models/README.md:62-63`.
- **What happens:** `sam3/job.py:53` stores `obj.mask_box_xyxy_px` (the tight bbox around all `True` pixels of the upsampled 288×288 mask) instead of `obj.detector_box_xyxy_px` (SAM3's single-object DETR regression box). When one DETR query leaks even a tiny stray mask island onto another person across the gym, `mask_to_box_xyxy_px` stretches the box across both people and all the empty floor between them.
- **Measured by decoding all 4,897 RLE masks in `20261126-130121-sam3.concept-7efc` (score > 0.5) using `src/rin/servants/sam3/.venv/bin/python`:**
  - **31 of all 43 deleted boxes (72%)** — and **25 of 25 deleted wide boxes (`w/h > 1.5`)** — have **≥ 20 px of completely empty columns or rows** splitting their mask in two. Examples deleted: `1772×203` px with **1,716 empty columns** (`fill = 0.006`, `score = 0.898`); `1757×354` px with **1,706 empty columns** (`fill = 0.011`, `score = 0.938`); `455×103` px with **411 empty columns** (`fill = 0.049`, `score = 0.898`, visible in `false_at_0.5.jpg` row 3 col 8).
  - **102 more boxes with ≥ 20 px completely empty row/column gaps** (29 with mask fill < 0.20, down to **0.055**) are **still in `71ed`** because the second pass (`wide?`) only checked `w/h > 1.5` or `h > 400`, missing square and tall disconnected boxes (e.g. `m6c8f324b5ece8690` f=`3750`: `95×169`, 67 empty cols / 84 empty rows, fill `0.055`; `m272a92d3c1b0e85c` f=`4425`: `206×141`, 173 empty cols, fill `0.064`; `m562f09e2fe07883d` f=`8760`: `217×241` and `241×241`, 135 and 150 empty cols, fill `0.075` and `0.089`).
  - Also still in `71ed`: **7 near-duplicate box pairs with IoU ≥ 0.8** (up to **0.966**, e.g. `2018-03-12 10:10 G299 f=8055`: `[1093,174,1121,209]` and `[1093,174,1122,209]`; `2018-03-11 11:20 G299 f=8070`: IoU `0.910`), **16 pairs with IoU ≥ 0.7**, and **187 pairs** where ≥90% of a smaller box lies inside a larger box.
- **Why this is critical for M4:** M4 trains YOLO on **unchecked** SAM3 boxes (`usage.md:247`). Leaving `sam3/job.py:53` on raw `mask_box_xyxy_px` will inject ~2–3% giant empty-floor-spanning boxes (at high confidence, `0.80–0.94`) directly into every training and val split in M4.
- **Fix before M4:**
  1. In `sam3/job.py`, evaluate `obj.detector_box_xyxy_px` vs `obj.mask_box_xyxy_px` on those 133 disconnected cases (the two are already provided by `sam3_engine`). For detection boxes, either use `detector_box_xyxy_px`, or keep only the mask connected component(s) overlapping `detector_box_xyxy_px` (or largest component) — and flag/drop masks with tiny fill ratio or huge `IoU(mask_box, detector_box)` mismatch.
  2. Clean the 102 disconnected-mask boxes, the 160 stationary bag boxes, and the ≥0.8 IoU duplicate pairs out of `71ed` (as a new checked run) before M5 ever scores against it.

## 2. Bugs in current code

### 2.1 `human.annotate` makes partial/multi-session review impossible when `start_from` is used (High)
- **Where:** `src/rin/human/annotate.py:63-68`.
- **What happens:**
  ```python
  unfinished = [(f["media_id"], f["frame_idx"]) for f in drawn["frames"] if f["boxes"] and done_tag not in f["tags"]]
  if unfinished:
      raise ValueError(...)
  ```
  When `viewer.show` is called with `start_from=sam` (D-33's standard workflow), **almost every frame starts pre-populated with SAM3 boxes**. If a person checks 100 of 300 frames, tags those 100 `done`, and tries to save a checkpoint run with `human.annotate`, it refuses because the other 200 unreviewed frames already have SAM3's starting boxes. The docstring (`annotate.py:15,23`) and `test_annotate_files_only_the_frames_tagged_done` (`test_viewer.py:108-114`) only work for blind drawing from scratch.
- **Reproduced:** In an isolated temp store with 3 images, boxes pre-populated on all 3, and only frame 0 tagged `done`, `human.annotate` raised `ValueError: 2 frames have boxes but no 'done' tag`.
- **Fix:** When `info.get("start_from")` is set (or via an explicit flag), only check untagged frames whose boxes *differ* from `start_from`, or file only `done`-tagged frames and warn about untagged ones.

### 2.2 `NaN` bypasses `instances.score` and `frame_scores.value` validation (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/store/schema.py:131-133` (and `conform` at `schema.py:96-117`).
- **What happens:**
  ```python
  scores = table.column("score").drop_null().to_numpy()
  if ((scores < 0) | (scores > 1)).any():
      raise ValueError("Table 'instances': score outside [0, 1]")
  ```
  In IEEE 754, `NaN < 0` and `NaN > 1` are both `False`, so `float('nan')` silently passes (unlike line 124's `~((x0 >= 0) & ...)` which *does* reject `NaN`). Similarly, `frame_scores.value` has no `isfinite` check in `conform()`.
- **Reproduced:** In an isolated temp store, writing `score=[float('nan')]` to `instances` and `value=[float('nan')]` to `frame_scores` both succeeded without error.
- **Fix:** Use `if (~((scores >= 0) & (scores <= 1))).any():` (or `~np.isfinite`), and check `np.isfinite` on `frame_scores.value` and `frames.pts`.

### 2.3 Durability gaps in `write_durably` and `RunWriter.__exit__` (Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/store/writer.py:82-89, 188-192`.
- **Two issues:**
  1. `write_durably` calls `_fsync(partial)` and `os.replace(partial, target)` but **never calls `_fsync(target.parent)`** (unlike `RunWriter.__exit__` at line 192), so a hard crash right after `os.replace` can still lose the directory entry. Also, if `write(partial)` raises mid-write, `partial` is **not cleaned up** (reproduced: `out.txt.<pid>.partial` remained on disk after `write()` raised).
  2. `RunWriter.__exit__` runs `for file in self.tmp_dir.iterdir(): _fsync(file)`. That only fsyncs top-level entries. As soon as M4's `export.yolo` and `yolo.train` write subdirectories (`files/images/`, `files/labels/`, `files/best.pt`, `data_model.md:184`), **no file inside `files/` will be fsynced before the atomic rename**. Use `rglob("*")` (files first, then directories bottom-up).

### 2.4 `human.annotate` timestamps miss the FiftyOne job and silently accept missing `dataset.info` (Low–Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/human/annotate.py:37-51`.
- **Issues:**
  1. `viewer.drawn_boxes(...)` runs *before* `with m.writer(...)`, so `finished_at - started_at` excludes the FiftyOne dump subprocess (in `workspace/store/`, `71ed` records `0.15 s`). Same bug fixed for other steps in M3 step 1 (from M2 audit §1.4).
  2. Line 40: `if info.get("frames", frames.id) != frames.id:` — if `dataset.info` is empty `{}` (e.g. a FiftyOne dataset not built by `viewer.show`), the default `frames.id` makes it silently pass (reproduced), losing all provenance of what the annotator saw. Fail loudly if `"frames"` is missing from `info`.

### 2.5 `viewer.show` collides field names when comparing two runs of the same servant with `label_scores` (Low–Medium)
- **Where:** `src/rin/viewer/__init__.py:89-95`.
- **What happens:** Without `label_scores`, field names include `run.id[-4:]` (`run_sam3_concept_7efc`). With `label_scores=(0.3, 0.5)`, field names are `hints_{run.step.split('.')[0]}_{band}` — **omitting `run.id[-4:]`**. Comparing two `sam3.concept` runs (e.g. different checkpoints or prompts) with a score band always crashes with `ValueError: Two label runs would share a field name`.
- **Reproduced:** Calling `viewer.show(m, fs, name='cmp', labels=[r1, r2], label_scores=(0.3, 0.5))` with two `sam3.concept` runs raised `ValueError`.

### 2.6 Minor code & provenance issues in `sam3` and `writer` (Low)
- **`src/rin/servants/sam3/job.py:48`:** Passes `replace_existing_objects=False`. Because `job.py` immediately extracts `obj` and never reads `session._objects` again, keeping existing objects retains full-res `bool` masks and `288×288` `float32` logits in RAM across all prompts for the entire chunk (`vendors/sam3_engine/src/sam3_engine/image_session.py:231-245`). Passing `replace_existing_objects=True` frees them immediately on multi-class runs.
- **`src/rin/servants/sam3/__init__.py:68`:** Calls `m.layout.relative_media_path(checkpoint)`, so a checkpoint outside `workspace/` raises a confusing error telling the user to `"Put media under the workspace (e.g. workspace/media/)"` (`src/rin/store/layout.py:55`).
- **`src/rin/servants/sam3/__init__.py:115`:** `report['seconds'] / len(images)` raises `ZeroDivisionError` if given an empty frame set.
- **`src/rin/store/writer.py:45-55` (`code_version`):** Only diffs `src/`, so uncommitted changes to root `pyproject.toml` or `uv.lock` are untracked by `src_dirty` / `src.diff`. Also note that all 14 runs in `workspace/store/` have `src_dirty: true` (each carrying a 30–40 KB `src.diff`), because experiments are run before committing; harmless now, but worth being aware of if M4/M5 writes 150+ runs while `src/` is dirty (and if `src/rin/servants/*/uv.lock` is ever modified while dirty, `src.diff` will duplicate megabytes of lockfile per run).

## 3. Traps ahead for M4 and M5

### 3.1 D-34 (`crowd` ignore regions) breaks the `usage.md` B4/C3 YOLO evaluation design
- **Where:** `dev/notes/decisions.md:86` (D-34), `dev/notes/usage.md:207,260`.
- **The mismatch:** `usage.md` exports the test set as YOLO (`export.yolo`) and scores via `yolo.evaluate`. D-34 correctly notes Ultralytics doesn't honour `iscrowd` and switches M5 evaluation to `pycocotools` — which changes the entire evaluation interface:
  1. You need a **COCO GT export** (or in-memory COCO GT dict) where `class == 'crowd'` boxes are emitted with **`category_id = person` and `iscrowd = 1`** (NOT as a separate `crowd` category, or `COCOeval` won't ignore `person` predictions inside them!).
  2. Student predictions on the test set must run at **low confidence** (e.g. `conf=0.001`, matching COCO/Ultralytics val protocol, not `yolo.predict`'s default `0.25`) so the PR curve isn't truncated.
  3. **Don't delete 20–40 individual boxes per crowd region by hand in FiftyOne** across 85 frames — that's tedious and guarantees missed leftovers violating D-34 (*"a region is scored person by person or ignored, not both"*). Draw only the `crowd` boxes in FiftyOne, and strip any `person` box whose containment inside a `crowd` box exceeds a clear threshold (e.g. ≥ 0.5) automatically when building the run or COCO GT, printing how many were dropped so the user can inspect them.
  4. Note also `COCOeval`'s default `maxDets = [1, 10, 100]` — fine for MEVA's max 70 people/frame, worth asserting `max_gt_per_frame <= 100`.

### 3.2 Unchecked SAM3 `pool` and `val` labels (D-31 + C2) will inherit both M3 SAM3 failure modes
- **Where:** `dev/notes/decisions.md:80` (D-31), `dev/notes/usage.md:240-247,255-260`.
- Before running `sam3.concept` on M4's pool and `val` split:
  1. Fix `sam3/job.py:53` (disconnected masks / `detector_box_xyxy_px`, §1.3 above) so unchecked training and val sets don't get ~2.7% giant empty-floor boxes.
  2. Check the chosen `val` hour (from 2018-03-07 hours 09, 11, or 17) for stationary false positives (like 03-13/03-15's bleacher bags) — Ultralytics picks `best.pt` on `val`, so systematic false positives in `val` bias checkpoint selection across every method.
  3. Also note `usage.md:256`'s placeholder gate `area BETWEEN 0.0001 AND 0.5`: in the checked test set, `0.0001 × 1920 × 1080 = 207 px²` drops 0 real people (smallest is ~300 px²), while 393 of 4,884 people (8.0%) are COCO-small (`< 32² = 1,024 px²`). Keep `mAP_small / medium / large` sliced in M5 (already kept from M2 audit §3.7).

### 3.3 Test-set temporal clustering reduces effective sample size below 300
- **Where:** `workspace/store/runs/20261126-130121-curate.random-0187`, `dev/notes/decisions.md:82` (D-32).
- **Measured on `0187`:** Drawing 300 frames uniformly at random from five 15-minute windows (150 camera-minutes total) produces **21 within-camera pairs ≤ 2 s apart** (min gap **0.5 s**!), **51 pairs ≤ 5 s apart**, and **24 G299 test frames within 2 s of a G330 test frame** (the same people in the same room at the same instant). Combined with ~111 truly empty frames, the 85 crowded frames come from just three 15-minute events (03-09, 03-11, 03-12). That doesn't invalidate the test set, but M5 shouldn't over-interpret small mAP differences (e.g. 1–2 points) as statistically meaningful.

## 5. Doubts about the direction and workflow

### 5.1 Pre-populating SAM3 boxes as an editable layer (`D-33`) failed its accuracy goal on the test set
- The blind pilot proved drawing from scratch is too slow (~7 s/box) and misses ~10% of people, so using SAM3 as an aid makes sense. **What failed was clicking through 300 pre-populated frames in 14 minutes (2.8 s/frame):** the reviewer's eye naturally scans for glaring errors (stretched boxes) and rubber-stamps stationary background false positives (bags on bleachers) and subtle misses/duplicates.
- Before spending GPU hours on M5, invest 20 minutes in **targeted, machine-assisted cleanup** of `71ed` (filed as a new checked run):
  1. **Stationary-box audit:** Group boxes by `(camera, day, rounded cx, cy)` — anything appearing at the same pixel spot across ≥50% of frames of a clip/day is almost certainly a bag, coat, or chair (catches all ~160 false positives on 03-13 and 03-15 in seconds!).
  2. **Disconnected-mask / low-fill audit:** Surface the 102 boxes in `71ed` whose SAM3 mask has a ≥20 px empty gap (especially the 29 with fill < 0.20) in FiftyOne.
  3. **Duplicate-box audit:** Surface the 16 pairs with IoU ≥ 0.7.
  4. **Blind-pilot reconciliation:** Reconcile the 18 pilot frames so known pilot errors (`03-13 G330 1680`, `5715`, `03-15 G330 6690`, `03-12 G299 4635`, etc.) are actually resolved.

### 5.2 Re-evaluate `review_by_exception.md` with temporal-consistency signals
- `review_by_exception.md:32` currently builds on the flawed "6 of 215" sparse-frame error rate (§1.1). Once `71ed` is cleaned, re-run that analysis — and add **two cheap signals that M3 already proved**:
  1. **Mask fill ratio / disconnectedness** (or `IoU(mask_box, detector_box)`): directly catches SAM3's group/stretched boxes.
  2. **Temporal stationarity on fixed cameras:** a high-confidence `person` box at identical coordinates across a whole window on an otherwise empty camera is a massive false-positive flag (bags/coats/chairs).
